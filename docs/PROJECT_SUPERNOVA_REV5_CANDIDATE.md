## 7.2 Compiler correctness

For every legal source strategy \(\pi\), there must exist a legal compiled strategy \(T(\pi)\) such that:

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm source}^{\pi}
(\tau_{\rm visible},R,C)
=
\mathcal L_{\rm compiled}^{T(\pi)}
(\tau_{\rm visible},R,C).
\]

Conversely, every legal compiled strategy relevant to optimisation must correspond to a legal source strategy.

Therefore:

\[
\sup_{\pi\in\Pi_{\rm source}}E_\pi[R-C]
=
\sup_{\pi'\in\Pi_{\rm compiled}}E_{\pi'}[R-C].
\]

Hence:

\[
\boxed{
OPT_{\rm source}=OPT_{\rm compiled}.
}
\]

Full abstraction is not required.

The required contract is:

- bidirectional legal-strategy correspondence;
- consumer-visible probabilistic trace preservation;
- reward preservation;
- complete-cost preservation.
