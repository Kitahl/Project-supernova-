## 1. Design freeze

No protocol 2.6 and no Revision 5 may become canonical until repository protection/source-bound admission is independently qualified and **two consecutive clean countable protocol-2.5 replay cohorts** close end-to-end. Pre-cohort defects are repaired as 2.5 hardening and frozen before countable cohort 1. Any authoritative change after cohort 1 begins resets the streak by default.

Allowed during the freeze: H0/H1 synthetic qualification, retrospective/non-admissible diagnostics, OCN-0A costing when source is available, theorem drafting, schema implementation and monotone rejection-only hardening. None changes scientific evidence status.

## 2. Authority

- **Math Foundry:** sole exact mathematical/runtime execution authority; exact legal actions, exact transformations, real probes, products and runtime receipts. It cannot self-admit a product.
- **Mastermind:** `PRE_REVIEW_ONLY`; bounded proposals, adversarial designs, falsifiers and plans. It cannot execute, self-grade or promote.
- **Tribunal / Librarian / Court:** scientific history/status, causal experiments, statement fidelity, M5 transfer, M7 revalidation, M8 interaction/locality challenge, M9 protected admission/lifetime risk, T3-RW and T5.
- **General-rho / SN-HORIZON:** finite-horizon decision and reasoning-computation allocation over registered evidence. It does not create legal actions or mathematical truth.
- **SN-CONSEQUENCE / SN-WORLD:** non-authoritative consequence estimates and optional calibrated bounds. They never execute, verify, admit or write scientific state.
- **GitHub / Scheduled Tasks:** transport, branch-isolated evidence, independent verification/integration routing and guarded control transitions only.

Binding state invariant:

`State_Supernova_control INTERSECT State_Tribunal_scientific = EMPTY`

Supernova control state may reference Tribunal scientific objects by immutable identity. It may not become a second Librarian.

## 3. Canonical definition and goals

Supernova is a frozen-authority controller that treats independently verified mathematical products as resources enabling further executable operations, gates continuation by certified value-of-computation, and admits improvements only through prospective matched-complete-cost tests.

- **Goal 1:** a finite, fresh, independently verified within-problem cascade beats strong controls at equal complete cost.
- **Goal 2:** after Goal 1, establish three distinct causal claims in order: `T3-RW -> T5 -> DR27/E5B`. Policy change is not benefit; memory benefit is not improver improvement. The improver claim requires `F`, `M` and `I` separation, the same untouched `F*`, matched complete R&D budget and fresh independent evaluation.
- **Ignition:** certified value-gated continuation, never `R>1` or another spectral criticality threshold.

## 4. Trusted Supernova Core

Ordered execution-control pipeline:

1. exact `OperatorContract` engine `E_Λ`;
2. sound certified viability/deadness and cost lower bounds;
3. verified-product enablement and direct-value priors;
4. lawful class-aware real multi-fidelity probes with successive halving;

Only stages 1–2 may hard-prune unconditionally. If an exact deterministic transformation is available, execute it rather than training a model to mimic it. A predicted lemma is hypothetical and never a `VerifiedProduct`.

## 5. SN-HORIZON Frontier

The Frontier has no execution authority. For finite state/action/horizon/budget/semantics, maintain typed intervals

`L_H(S,a;b,Λ) <= Q*_H(S,a;b,Λ) <= U_H(S,a;b,Λ)`.

Evidence kinds:

- `SOUND` — certificates, exact witnesses/search or proved cost bounds; may support certified stops within frozen scope.
- `ANYTIME_CALIBRATED(alpha)` — valid time-uniform sequential guarantee.
- `FIXED_SAMPLE_CALIBRATED(alpha)` — valid only for its preregistered design/population/cutoff.
- `HEURISTIC_ONLY` — may prioritize the next computation; **never** narrows a certifying interval or supports a stop.

Intersection rules: SOUND/SOUND is unconditional; SOUND/calibrated inherits calibrated risk; calibrated/calibrated requires a valid joint certificate or conservative common alpha ledger. Overlapping sources are never treated as independent confirmations. Statistical intervals may widen after recalibration or shift detection. Persistence over updates is a stability heuristic, not a coverage theorem.

Stop classes:

1. certified: `L_SOUND(A) > max_B!=A U_SOUND(B) + delta`;
2. risk-limited: simultaneously valid calibrated intervals separate the action under declared lifetime/family risk;
3. no certifiable stop: compute again, fall back or exhaust the metalevel budget.

Positive termination requires evidence that raises a lower bound; elimination alone is insufficient.

### Metalevel computation controls

- `M0`: cheapest admissible probe;
- `M1`: fixed computation order;
- `M2`: myopic value of computation;
- `M3`: learned computation selector;
- `M*`: exact tiny-world oracle.

Every choice emits a `ComputationSelectionTrace`; unequal evidence allocation is audited by Tribunal. Metalevel cost is hard-capped and included in complete cost.

### Frontier evidence order

`sound bounds -> real probes -> SN-COMPARE -> one-step SN-CONSEQUENCE -> direct horizon/plan prediction -> short residual SN-WORLD`.

SN-WORLD is a consequence backend, not another executive. Run ORACLE-WORLD and HEADROOM-0 before serious world-model training. Core admission requires SNW-0 through SNW-5; only SNW-5 supports operationally useful multi-step modelling.

## 6. Global design invariants

- target quantities are finite horizon `H`, budget `b`, frozen `Λ`, or diagnostic only;
- relaxed/static positive reachability is `NO_INFORMATION`; only sound negative certificates may hard-prune;
- kill/defer learned mechanisms on residual-value UCB versus unavoidable-cost LCB + deployment margin;
- report predictive accuracy, calibration/risk coverage and usable intervention coverage for every learned component; near-total abstention is failure;
- every gate records point estimate, CI, MDE, n, achieved power, complete cost, family heterogeneity and zero/unknown counts;
- fidelity/checker costs are symmetric across applicable arms;
- `KERNEL_VALID` and `STATEMENT_FIDELITY_*` are orthogonal;
- **no receipt is not a null result**; a no-finding run requires an explicit terminal zero-delta receipt;
- multiple checkers are defence-in-depth, not statistical independence;
- learn only residual uncertainty after exact Foundry semantics;
- query before model: the `QuerySpec` determines witness class, policy family, horizon/risk and cheapest qualified backend.

## 8. CI and TCB

Three status contexts are non-substitutable:

- `supernova/static-control` — frozen protocol/control/assignment/schema/dependency/action identities and generation invariants;
- `supernova/report-admission` — post-write MM06 verifier-head admission;
- `supernova/transition-admission` — BIL00 transition/CAS/allowed-diff admission.

Missing, pending, failing, unobservable, wrong-source or wrong-commit is not PASS. MM06 cannot self-attest future CI; MF06 must later observe `report-admission=success` on the exact verifier head. Countable workflows pin Actions by full commit SHA and freeze direct/transitive dependencies by hashes or an equivalent immutable environment receipt.

Every fresh/scientifically model-sensitive receipt records requested model/effort and observed binding status. A request string is not a binding receipt. Fresh G1/G8/Goal-2 evidence is non-promotable without the frozen model/tools/environment binding required by the manifest.

Verifier assurance is typed: primary kernel, same-kernel fresh replay, external implementation, statement identity checker and statement-fidelity authority. Default independence status is `NO_INDEPENDENCE_CLAIM`. Exact versions, shared TCB, advisories, exploit regressions and mutation/fuzz evidence are recorded. Same-kernel replay is never labelled external verification.

## 9. Liveness
